VoiceVault PDF Japanese fonts

The static Regular and Bold TrueType files in the application are derived from
Noto Sans JP by instantiating its wght axis at 400 and 700. The family and
PostScript names were changed to VoiceVault PDF Japanese names for this
modified version.
