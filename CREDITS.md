# Thank you

VoiceVault stands on the work of many people who chose to share it. Thank you to every team below. Their models, code and data make this app possible. Each keeps its own licence; see [docs/MODEL_LICENSES.md](docs/MODEL_LICENSES.md) for the licences of the models.

## Voice and speech models

- **Fish Audio**: Drama 3, VoiceVault's main voice. *Built with Fish Audio.*
- **hexgrad**: Kokoro 82M, the fast chat voice.
- **Maya Research**: Maya1, voices designed from a description; **Hubert Siuzdak**: the SNAC audio codec it uses.
- **k2-fsa / Next-gen Kaldi**: OmniVoice.
- **Microsoft**: VibeVoice.
- **MediaTek Research / BreezeBlue**: Breeze TTS 2.
- **OpenAI**: Whisper speech recognition.
- **Qwen team (Alibaba)**: Qwen3-ASR and the Qwen3 forced aligner.
- **OpenMOSS**: MOSS-Transcribe-Diarize, which tells speakers apart.
- **Plachtaa**: Seed-VC voice conversion.
- **Sidon** and **UniverSR** authors: speech restoration and bandwidth extension.
- **Meta AI**: SAM-Audio and Demucs (HTDemucs stem separation).
- **Mel-Band RoFormer** authors and the community separation projects.

## Music and sound

- **ACE Studio and StepFun**: ACE-Step 1.5.
- **Multimodal Art Projection (M-A-P)**: YuE2.
- **Chris Donahue et al.**: SheetSage.
- **Stability AI**: Stable Audio 3.

## Runtime and tools

- **audio.cpp** (ShugoAI): the engine that runs the GGUF models on any GPU or CPU.
- **eSpeak NG**: pronunciation for Kokoro.
- **FFmpeg**: audio and video decoding.
- **yt-dlp**: fetching public media for Harvest.
- **PyTorch**, **Hugging Face Transformers and Hub**, **librosa**, **NumPy**, **SciPy**, **soundfile**, **pyworld** (WORLD vocoder).
- **FastAPI**, **Uvicorn**, **Pydantic**, **HTTPX**, **Prisma**.
- **React**, **Vite**, **Tailwind CSS**, **Electron**, **Radix UI**, **dnd-kit**, **Zustand**, **Lucide** icons, **jsPDF**, **html2canvas**.
- **uv** (Astral): sets up Python for the installer.

## Catalogue data

Discover's shows, films, people, games and characters come from:
- **TMDB**: *This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.*
- **IGDB** (Twitch).
- **AniList**.
- **Wikidata**.

Cast's Craft with AI reads character articles from **Wikipedia**, whose text is available under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Each excerpt is kept with a link to its article.

Titles, names and images belong to their owners. VoiceVault only displays them for browsing and organising, and claims no affiliation.
